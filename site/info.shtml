c o m p e t i t i o n s
Of course we will organize competitions. Make sure you've read these general competition rules and the part about submitting your entry.
Entries may not have been released or made publicly accessible before LTP'3.

Entries that show an obvious lack of quality, are insulting to other people, or break the French laws will be disqualified.

Competitors don't have to be physically present at the party place in order to participate but can't ask for prices.

A jury will make a pre-selection if there are too many entries entered for a competition.

If there are not enough entries for a competition, the competition will be canceled.

Deadlines will be published on the LTP'3 website. Competitors are allowed to submit entries after the deadlines, but the organizers can not guarantee that these entries will be included in the competitions.

Prizes will be handed out at the party place. Competitors that won a prize will loose their rights to this prize if they are not present during the prize ceremony.

The LTP'3 organization has the right to spread all entries on CD-ROM, via internet, or any other electronic or non-electronic media.
