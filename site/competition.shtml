PC demo competition
Maximum Time : 15 minutes.
Maximum compressed demo size : 8 megabytes (8,388,608 bytes).
The demo MUST play sound.

Demos are allowed to use hardware accelerated 3D in this competition.

Oldschool demo competition

Maximum Time : 15 minutes
Maximum runnable demo will be : no size limitation (only memory limitation)
The demo MUST play sound.

All Oldschool demos must be launched via 3"1/2 disk but could be copied on Hard drive.

WARNING : NO OTHER COMPUTER ALLOWED HERE !

PC 64kb intro competition

Maximum Time : 10 minutes.
Maximum runnable intro size is 64 kilobytes (65,536 bytes), Dos-extender inside.
The intro MUST play sound.

You aren't allowed to use hardware accelerated 3D in this competition.

PC 4kb intro competition

Each intro will be shown for a maximum of 5 minutes.
Maximum runnable intro size is 4 kilobytes (4096 bytes).
The intro MUST play sound.

You aren't allowed to use hardware accelerated 3D in this competition.

***
Games must run under DOS, Windows or Linux.

Game compo

Multi-channels Tracker music competition

Only .MOD, .XM, .IT, .S3M, .MT2 format will be allowed.

Each song will be played for a maximum of 4 minutes.
Maximum size is 1.39 megabytes (1,457,664 bytes) (must fit in a floppy without being zipped or rared or anything else ;).

4 Channels Tracker music competition

Only .MOD tracker-based music will be allowed.

Each song will be played for a maximum of 4 minutes.
Maximum size is 512 kilobytes (524,288 bytes) !

MP3 Music Competition

Only .MP3 music will be allowed.

Each song will be played for a maximum of 4 minutes.


ChipTune music competition

Only .MOD .XM .IT tracker-based music will be allowed.
