08/19/99

TABLE RESERVATION

To reserve a table just send an e-mail to us with these information :
- a unique group name (we don't want two reservations for one group)
- a list of people who are in the REGISTRATION page
- Number of computers for each person.

Be careful to not write the name of someone who is included in another reservation.
